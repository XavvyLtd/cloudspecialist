Engagement Flow

From first conversation to a running platform.

A single session with both teams. Xavvy frames the business outcomes and constraints, and we look at the actual estate: clusters, bills and pipelines.

STEP 2

You get a single proposal with clear scope for each partner and shared milestones, rather than two contracts to reconcile.
