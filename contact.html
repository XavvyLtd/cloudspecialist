Before You Write

Common questions.

Will you sign an NDA before we share details?

Yes. We're happy to sign your NDA, or send ours, before any architecture, billing or code is shared.

Do you only take on large, long-running engagements?

No. Many engagements start with a focused two-week diagnostic and cost audit. You get concrete findings either way, and you decide if you want to go further.

Do you work alongside our existing engineering team?

Always. We work in your repositories with your engineers, and we hand over full ownership of the code, runbooks and documentation. The goal is a team that doesn't need us.

Which clouds and platforms do you cover?

AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and Cloudflare, with Kubernetes, Terraform and GPU/LLM serving stacks on top. We're vendor-neutral and recommend whatever fits your workload and budget.
